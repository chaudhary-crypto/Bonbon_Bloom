Privacy Policy for Bonbon Bloom

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Bonbon Bloom, operated by Novaro Technologies. This Privacy Policy describes how our puzzle game collects, processes, stores, and protects your data when you play offline or sign in for cloud save and purchases.

1. Offline Guest Play vs. Cloud Sign-In

You can play Bonbon Bloom as an offline Guest, storing progression and settings locally inside an encrypted-signature Room SQLite database on your device. If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager), your profile, level progress, and verified purchases synchronize over TLS with Google Cloud Firestore.

2. Data Stored & Processed

  • Account Identifiers (Optional Google Sign-In): Firebase Authentication UID, display name, Google account email address, and profile avatar URL strictly to restore your progress and verify purchase ownership across devices.
  • Gameplay Progression & Anti-Cheat Integrity: Completed levels (1–1,000), star ratings, high scores, booster counts, preferences (sound, music, haptics, language), and HMAC-SHA256 integrity signatures.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: Handled exclusively by Google Play Billing Library 8.0.0. We never receive or store credit card, UPI, or banking numbers. We process Play Order IDs, Product SKUs, and purchase tokens solely for server-side verification, fraud prevention, and entitlement restoration.
  • Advertising & Privacy Consent (AdMob + UMP): Non-paying ad-supported players may see ads served via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which may process Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and diagnostic telemetry. EEA/UK/California players are shown Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before ad requests are sent and can revise consent anytime in Settings > Privacy Options.
  • Local Notifications: Scheduled on-device via Android WorkManager.

3. Zero-Trust Cloud Security

All cloud transfers are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA / Play Integrity protects backend purchase verification and database endpoints from unauthorized access.

4. Children's Privacy

Bonbon Bloom is intended for a General Audience (Ages 13 and older) and is not directed to children under 13 (or applicable age of digital consent under GDPR/DPDP). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Your Rights & Permanent Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account, local Room database, Cloud Firestore profile, purchase ledger associations, and Firebase Authentication credentials at any time:

  • In-Game Instant Deletion: Open Settings > Delete Account & Data.
  • Web Deletion Portal: Submit a request at delete-account.html.
