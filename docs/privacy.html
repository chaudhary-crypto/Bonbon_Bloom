Privacy Policy for Bonbon Bloom

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Bonbon Bloom! This Privacy Policy describes how our match-3 puzzle game collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Offline-First Privacy Architecture

Bonbon Bloom is engineered to be completely playable offline. We believe in privacy by default: your game saves, level progression, stars, high scores, coins, gems, and inventory stay securely on your physical device.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Progress & Inventory: Completed levels (1–1,000), star ratings, booster counts, streak progress, and settings preferences (audio, music, haptics, colorblind mode).
  • Anti-Tamper Integrity: Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 checksums to verify balance integrity without transmitting user data over the internet.

3. Third-Party Services (When Online)

When your device is connected to the internet, the following Google Play-certified SDKs may process diagnostic or interaction data:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to deliver optional banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. May process advertising IDs, IP addresses, and diagnostic crash data. Users in the EEA/UK can adjust consent anytime via in-game Settings > Privacy Options.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to securely fulfill in-app purchases (coins, boosters, Remove Ads) and subscriptions (VIP Gold Pass) without sharing credit card numbers with us.
  • Android WorkManager: Runs locally on your device to schedule battery-friendly notifications (e.g. lives restored, daily rewards).

4. Children's Privacy

Bonbon Bloom is rated for a General Audience (Ages 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion ("Delete My Data")

You have full ownership of your data. You can erase all locally stored data, balances, and achievements at any time directly in the game by going to Settings > Delete My Data.
