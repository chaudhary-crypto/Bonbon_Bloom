# Privacy Policy for Bonbon Bloom

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Welcome to **Bonbon Bloom**! This Privacy Policy explains how our match-3 puzzle mobile game ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our Android application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
Bonbon Bloom is engineered with an **offline-first** design. You can play all levels, unlock chapters, spin the Lucky Wheel, and customize your garden without an active internet connection.

#### A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
- **Game Progress & Balance**: Completed levels, star ratings, high scores, coins, gems, booster counts, streak progress, and settings preferences (sound, music, haptics, colorblind mode).
- **Anti-Cheat Validation**: Cryptographic HMAC hashes stored in private app sandbox storage.

#### B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs
When your device is connected to the internet, certain third parties may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data:
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: Device identifiers (Advertising ID), IP address, crash logs, and ad interaction metrics to serve non-personalized and personalized banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
- **Google Play Billing**: Purchase tokens, order IDs, and subscription entitlement status to verify in-app purchases and subscriptions.
- **Google Play Services / WorkManager**: Anonymous device hardware diagnostics to schedule energy-efficient local notifications (stamina refills, daily streak reminders).

---

### 2. How We Use Information
We use information solely to:
- Maintain your game saves, inventory, and unlocked levels.
- Deliver rewarded video ads (e.g. +5 moves or free Lucky Wheel spins) when you voluntarily choose to watch them.
- Fulfill in-app purchases (coins, booster packs, VIP membership).
- Ensure game stability and prevent illegal memory tampering or fraud.

---

### 3. Children's Privacy
Bonbon Bloom is targeted at a general audience aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

---

### 4. User Controls & Data Rights
- **Delete My Data**: You can wipe all local game progress, inventory, and achievements at any time by opening **Settings > Delete My Data**.
- **Ad Privacy & Consent Options**: Players in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom can change or withdraw consent at any time via **Settings > Privacy Options**.
- **Opt Out of Personalized Ads**: You can reset or opt out of your Google Advertising ID in Android Settings under **Google > Ads**.
