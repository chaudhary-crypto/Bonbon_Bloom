User Account & Associated Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with the Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policy, GDPR, CCPA, and India's DPDP Act, players of Bonbon Bloom can permanently delete their account and associated cloud records at any time.

⚠️ Irreversible Action: Deleting your account permanently erases your level progression (Levels 1–1000), stars, high scores, coin & gem balances, booster inventory, active local entitlements, and Firebase Authentication profile. Note: deleting your game account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play recurring subscription — please also cancel subscriptions via Google Play Store > Payments & subscriptions.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open Bonbon Bloom on your Android device.
  2. Tap Settings in the top header.
  3. Tap "Delete Account & Data" and confirm.
  4. Your local Room database, Cloud Firestore document, subcollections, and Firebase Auth account are wiped immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you already uninstalled the app, submit the form below to queue an automated deletion of your cloud records within 24–48 business hours:

Data Types Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication Identity: UID, email reference, and linked Google Sign-In profile.
  • Cloud Firestore Records: /users/{uid}, level_progress, purchases, ledger, and reward_requests.
  • Local SQLite Room Database: Player profile, level progress, and cached purchase records.
